1. Executive Summary

Dravya AI Engine is a PyTorch and FastAPI-powered microservice designed for automated visual identity verification of raw Ayurvedic medicinal plant species.

  • Problem Solved: Eliminates species adulteration and substitution in raw herb harvests (e.g. Saraca asoca vs Polyalthia longifolia).
  • Input: Field RGB plant photos (JPEG/PNG/WebP, max 10MB).
  • Output: JSON payload with predicted species name, scientific botanical name, canonical ID, and top-5 confidence scores.
  • System Fit: Ingestion & Quality Gate layer before blockchain ledger logging.
  • Accuracy: 98.67% test accuracy across 82 species on held-out GPU evaluation set.
SIH Presentation Pitch
"Dravya AI automates botanical species identification with 98.67% accuracy across 82 Ayurvedic species. High-confidence predictions expedite blockchain batch logging, while low-confidence samples route to authorized government botanists for verification."
