Problem & Industry Bottleneck

Problem Statement
  • 15-20% Raw Drug Adulteration: Commercial supply chains suffer from species substitution due to visually identical lookalikes (e.g. Genuine Ashoka Saraca asoca vs False Ashoka Polyalthia longifolia).
  • Expert Scarcity: Manual botanical taxonomy inspection cannot scale to inspect thousands of daily field harvests.
  • Field Lighting & Camera Variances: Images taken by farmers exhibit shadow, leaf damage, and background soil clutter.
  • Solution: Dravya AI provides an objective 24/7 automated identification gate with softmax confidence scoring.

Data Pipeline & Cleanliness

MLOps & Audit
  • Read-Only Source Scanning: CIMPd, Hugging_Face, and Kaggle raw datasets are kept 100% immutable.
  • SHA-256 Duplicate Audit Engine: Eliminates duplicate image hashes cross-dataset to prevent train/test data leakage.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Review Queue: Candidate plant groups are reviewed via an append-only human review protocol.
  • Canonical Manifest Export: Clean dataset manifests lock exact image lineages to model version checkpoints.

SIH Defense & Q&A Cheat Sheet

SIH Presentation
  • Why EfficientNet-B0? Optimal parameter efficiency (5.3M params) and fast CPU inference (~45ms) with 98.67% accuracy.
  • What if AI is wrong? Dravya AI is a Decision Support System; low-confidence samples route to Government Botanists.
  • Why Blockchain + AI? AI classifies image patterns off-chain; Blockchain permanently logs immutable model signatures and hashes on-chain.
